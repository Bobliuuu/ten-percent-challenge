# Notes from v2/engine (14B) for altpush
- Rotation: block-128 sign-randomized Hadamard on the input dim of every linear (fold back after GPTQ for eval; seed is free). Without it symmetric grids collapse (14B L=8: 0.44 vs 0.76).
- Entropy-coded uniform grid (ECSQ) with the step chosen per group by MSE search over absmax*p, p in [0.25,1]; entropy of index histogram per module counts, not log2 L. Count scales (fp16/g) and ~0.5% ANS overhead.
- Allocation: per-module table (rel Hessian-weighted error vs entropy at several steps), Lagrangian pick. Pattern at 1.7 bpw (14B): v_proj, k_proj, o_proj high precision (6-16 levels), gate_proj low (2-3), up/down mid. Early layers (0-3) and last layers get more bits.
- Layer drop (block-influence cosine, never first 2/last 1) at equal bytes beat plain lower bits: drop6+L5 0.703 vs L4 0.662 at ~0.1175 ratio. Try combining drop with the allocation and dropping FFN of weakest layers only.
- To try next: (a) GPTQ-aware step choice that minimizes err+lam*entropy jointly rather than MSE alone; (b) rotate output dim too (QuIP-style two-sided) and share scales via larger g (g256 costs ~0.4pt for -0.003 ratio); (c) per-layer table with sequential-GPTQ errors instead of fp-input errors; (d) lattice/E8 or trellis codebook for the low-rate modules (2-3 levels) where scalar ECSQ loses ~0.25 bit to the RD bound; (e) bigger calibration (pool.jsonl) since sub-2bpw is calibration-limited; (f) lower ebits/vocab for embeddings (emb ~4% of budget at 14B, more at 3B).
- Gotchas: Modal detached local entrypoints die with the client; use `modal deploy` + Function.spawn (launch.py). A100-80GB queue can starve; fallback GPU list helps.
