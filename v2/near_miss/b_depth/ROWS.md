# b_depth near-miss rows (Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct, dev, real greedy GEN via vLLM)
Method family: whole-layer drop (single-layer task-NLL ablation, no training) + RTN group-128 quant (per-group MSE clip search, no weight update) + task vocab trim (tokens seen in calibration + specials + bytes; embed/head 4-bit). Calibration disjoint from eval. Quantisation/trim are SIMULATED (dequantised bf16, zeroed rows + allowed_token_ids); bytes are computed analytically (scales+zeros, trimmed tokenizer vocab+merges+8KB). No packed artifact was built.
Base accuracy on dev: EASY(SKILL7) .959 (n=682), T1 .728 (600), TOX .609 (705), INJ .827 (695). Retention = P(cand correct | base correct), Wilson 95% lower bound in brackets. Rows with size >9.9% are labelled by size.
Findings: (1) vocab trim from S1-calibration alone already costs retention (T1 .46, TOX .88, INJ .94 at fp16) because 65% of eval prompts contain OOV tokens (S3/OOD source): HIGH RISK on maintainer OOD data. (2) RTN 3-bit keeps EASY (.976) but is 18% size; RTN 2-bit g128 collapses everything. (3) dropping >=12 of 48 layers destroys the model even at fp16. Nothing reaches <=9.9% with usable retention; the <=9.9% points (d12 q2 9.43%, d18 q2 7.94%, d24 q3 9.26%) retain ~0 on EASY/TOX/INJ (T1 .39 = nocall-majority artefact).
